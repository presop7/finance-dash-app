Privacy Policy

Fi-Track · Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Fi-Track app ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and email address from your Google account (the "email" and "profile" permissions). We do not receive your Google password.
  • Financial records you enter: transactions (title, amount, date, note), categories, funds and your app settings (such as currency and date format).
  • Feedback you choose to send: the text and any pictures you attach, plus basic app and device information to help diagnose problems.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not access your bank accounts.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To store and sync your financial records across your sessions and devices.
  • To show your name in the app's greeting.
  • To read and respond to feedback you send.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing. Information received from Google is used only to provide sign-in and to display your name, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Your data is processed by these service providers on our behalf:

  • Supabase — account sign-in and the database (EU region, Ireland).
  • Render — the app's server (EU region, Frankfurt).
  • Resend — delivers feedback messages you send to us by email.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each account can only access its own records. Some app data is also cached on your device so the app works offline.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual records in the app at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below and we will do so within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it is processed, by contacting us. If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
