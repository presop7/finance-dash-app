Delete your Fi-Track account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Fi-Track and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm twice.

Your account and all your data are deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email [contact email] from the address you sign in with, asking to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and sign-in).
  • Everything you entered: transactions, categories, funds, savings goals, reminders, trackers and settings.
  • Notification registrations and feedback linked to your account.

Nothing is kept afterwards, except short-lived server logs (up to 30 days) and records we must keep by law, if any. A one-way fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of your email address, which can't be turned back into the address, is also kept: it only records that this email already had the free Premium trial, so it isn't given again (legitimate interest: preventing misuse of the free trial).

Subscriptions

A Premium subscription is managed by Google Play: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting your account doesn't stop billing.
