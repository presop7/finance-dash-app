Fi-Track

Personal finance, simply tracked.

Fi-Track is a mobile app for keeping track of your own money: record income and expenses, organise them into categories and funds, and see where your money goes with charts and insights.

  • Add, edit and categorise transactions — works offline and syncs when you're back online
  • Balances per fund (cash, bank accounts, savings)
  • Analytics with filters, category breakdowns and spending insights
  • Budget alerts and daily reminders

You can sign in with your email and password or with your Google account. Your data is private to your account.
