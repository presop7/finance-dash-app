Who we are

Fi-Track is provided by [your name / company name, address]. Contact: [contact email]. We are the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we collect

  • Account: your email address; with Google sign-in also your name. We never receive your Google password.
  • What you enter: transactions (title, amount, date, note), categories, funds, savings goals and the money set aside for them, reminders, trackers and app settings (currency, language, formats).
  • Subscription: if you buy Premium, Google Play handles the payment. We receive only the subscription status (plan, start, renewal or end date, trial status). We never see your card details.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a technical address your device or browser gives us for delivering notifications, plus your reminder times and time zone.
  • Feedback you send: the text, any pictures you attach, and basic app and device information.
  • Technical logs: server logs (IP address, time, request), kept briefly for security and troubleshooting.

We do not show ads, use advertising or analytics trackers, sell your data, or access your bank accounts.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • Providing the app and syncing it across your devices: our contract with you.
  • Premium and its trial: our contract with you.
  • Reminders and notifications: your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings or in your device's settings.
  • Offers inside the app about Premium (at most one a day): our legitimate interest. They stop once you have Premium.
  • Security and fixing problems: our legitimate interest.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), [Supabase region]
  • Render (our server), [Render region]
  • Google (sign-in, Google Play billing, notification delivery)
  • [subscription service, e.g. RevenueCat] (subscription status)
  • [Gmail / Resend] (account emails)

When you convert your currency, the app asks open.er-api.com for exchange rates. Only the currency codes are sent, no personal data. Where a provider is outside the EU, transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Server logs are kept up to 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account removes your account and all your data at once. Without the app: see how to delete your account, or email [contact email]; we act within 30 days. A Premium subscription has to be cancelled separately in Google Play: deleting the account doesn't stop billing. A one-way fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of your email address, which can't be turned back into the address, is also kept: it only records that this email already had the free Premium trial, so it isn't given again (legitimate interest: preventing misuse of the free trial).

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or export your data, object to its processing, and withdraw consent, by contacting us. In the EU you may also complain to your data protection authority; in Bulgaria, the Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg).

Children

Fi-Track is not intended for anyone under 16.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and every account can only reach its own records.

Changes
