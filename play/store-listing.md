**Short description** (78)
Track income, spending and savings. Goals, reminders and reports, made simple.

**Full description**

Fi-Track shows you where your money goes, with no spreadsheets and no bank connection needed.

LOG IN SECONDS
• Add an expense or income in a few taps: amount, category, fund (cash, bank…).
• Works offline: everything syncs by itself when you're back online.
• Your data on all your devices: phone and web.

SEE THE BIG PICTURE
• A dashboard with your balance, recent transactions and funds.
• Analytics by category, filtered by period, category and fund.
• A monthly report: how the month went, where you spend most, what changed.

SAVE FOR GOALS
• Savings goals: set money aside from your funds without moving it.
• See how much is set aside, and where the money is.
• Trackers for loans and money you lent.
